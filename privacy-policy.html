Privacy Policy for Sheen

Package: com.clicrore.sheen • Developer: Clicrore Apps • Effective Date: November 28, 2026

Sheen ("we", "our", or "the app") is a 100% offline Zikr and Tasbih counter designed with privacy at its core. We do not collect, store, sell, or share any personal information.

1. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

All Zikr titles, target counts, repetition logs, daily/hourly statistics, round timings, and appearance/sound settings are stored strictly locally on your device using Android's internal storage (Room Database and DataStore). We have zero access to this data.

2. Local JSON Backups

When you choose to export or import a backup file, Sheen uses Android's system file picker to read or write a local JSON file to the location you select. Backup files are never uploaded to our servers.

3. No Analytics, Ads, or Third-Party Tracking

Sheen does not contain advertisements, third-party analytics SDKs, crash-reporting trackers, or user accounts, and does not use the Android Advertising ID.

4. Device Permissions

Sheen only requests the VIBRATE permission to provide optional haptic feedback when counting.

5. Children's Privacy

Because Sheen collects no personal data of any kind, no information from children or adults is ever gathered or transmitted.

6. Changes to This Policy

Any future updates to this Privacy Policy will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

7. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact Clicrore Apps via our repository at github.com/museverse9/sheen-zikr-app.
